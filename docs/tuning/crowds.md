## Crowds

Ran out is how often anybody's patience ran out; it comes to a row, a heckle at somebody fighting, a new brawl, joining one, a quarrel the boss averted, or nothing (seethed). Dragged in is a fighter turning on somebody watching; piled in is somebody joining a fight before their patience ran out, and a boss stopping that counts as averted (pile-ins). Fighting is the share of the shift with a brawl going and, while one is, the share of the room on their feet in one; fights at once and the largest fight are the most there ever were. Kicked to death is killed after being carried out. Down is everybody carried out or killed by the end, of the room; half down is when half the room was, in the shifts it happened in.
